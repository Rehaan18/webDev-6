OUR STORY

Born from a love of great food

Cravings started in 2022 with a simple mission — to connect people with the food they love. What began as a small idea quickly grew into a platform that supports restaurants, riders, and customers alike.

Today, we continue to build a community where food brings people together — faster, fresher, and better than ever.
